# ADR-087 — Establish exeris-bot as the CI Publication and Capture Step (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-docs/adr/ADR-087-establish-exeris-bot-as-the-ci-publication-and-capture-step.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-docs/blob/main/adr/ADR-087-establish-exeris-bot-as-the-ci-publication-and-capture-step.md)

ADR-087 establishes `exeris-bot` and `exeris-inbox` as GitHub App identities and reusable workflow steps for publication and capture in CI.

## Why this stub exists

ADR-087 §B.6 and §B.6a directly govern this repository's decision schema contracts:
- The CI publish step consumes `verdict.base.schema.json` from the `exeris-agents` bundle composed in `.github`.
- The composed schema extends `findings.items` with an optional `tag` field for severity classifications.
- `exeris-agents` 2.0.0 moves closers from the base to compositions to permit this extension.
- The publish step enforces SemVer compatibility across `.agents/manifest.yaml` pins.

ADR-087's Engineering Protocol item 8 calls for this stub by name.
